## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Distinctive and topic-specific, but the first screen currently reads more like a persuasive campaign than a research archive. The search action and subject matter are relevant, while the bold defense-oriented promise does not clarify whether ADAM is primarily an archive, apologetics resource, or both.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI `detect --json` exited 0 with 0 findings in each of Hero.tsx, TopicsGrid.tsx, and ArticleSection.tsx. ## Overall Impression

The hero is polished and immediately searchable, but it makes a visitor infer the archive’s scope and what “answer” means. The strongest discovery content—categories and recent articles—arrives only after a full-screen hero.

## What's Working

- Search is the clearest action, with a labeled input and a concrete example query.
- The category grid and latest-article shelf provide useful discovery paths once reached.
- The mobile hero wraps without horizontal overflow, and the CTA remains full-width and easy to tap.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] The search CTA promises an answer, not article discovery.** The form routes to `/search?q=...`, where users browse article results. Rename it to “Pretraži arhivu” or make the destination genuinely answer-oriented. Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`.
2. **[P2] The four topic pills look interactive but do nothing.** They are plain spans in `Hero.tsx`. Link them to relevant categories or prefilled searches; otherwise remove the control-like styling. Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`.
3. **[P2] The full-height hero hides the archive’s breadth.** `min-h-[100svh]` keeps the category grid and recent articles out of the initial viewport, especially on mobile. Shorten the hero so the next section peeks into view and consider reducing the metric cards’ visual weight. Suggested command: `/impeccable layout`.
4. **[P2] Advocacy framing may conflict with the research-reference promise.** “Odbrani islama” and “organizovane sumnje” communicate a strong stance before the visitor sees the source-focused archive. Confirm that this is the intended primary positioning; if not, lead with the archive’s actual scope and evidence approach. Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`.
