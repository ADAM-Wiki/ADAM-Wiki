# Brag Plan: Adam-Wiki (ADAM — istraživačka baza znanja)

## What is this app?

A Bosnian/Serbian-language research archive — 204 articles across 8 active categories on Quranic preservation, hadith science, comparative religion and history, with instant full-text search over the whole archive, built as a quiet, serious reading room rather than a blog.

## The angle

An archive that treats itself like a manuscript. Ink-black paper, antique-bronze marginalia, one enormous faint `Å` behind the hero. The video plays it completely straight: no hype, no SaaS language — the site's own words do the bragging, ending on its best line, "u doba organizovane sumnje" (in the age of organized doubt).

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

The hero headline slams into a near-black frame: eyebrow `NAUČNA BAZA ZNANJA` ruled on both sides, then serif **Uspostavljanje *Istine* kroz dokaze** with *Istine* in bronze italic. No logo first — the sentence is the brand.

## Key moments (the middle)

- The search field typing its own placeholder: `Meka i Jesrib u Bibliji`, then the results panel landing with a bronze-highlighted match and `Pronađeno u tekstu`.
- Category cards arriving one by one with their real counts — KURAN 55 · ISTORIJA 41 · ATEIZAM 40 · OPORGOVANJE 24 · SPISI 18 · AHMEDIJE 11 · HADISKE NAUKE 9 · HRIŠĆANSTVO 6 — under the hero's own stat line, `204 članka · 8 kategorija`.

## Outro / punchline

The tagline's last clause lands alone, huge, in serif italic: **"u doba organizovane sumnje."** Then the ADAM wordmark and `adam-wiki.github.io/ADAM-Wiki` in mono.

## User worth showing

entry (hero search box) → key action (type a query, submit) → result (search results + preview panel with matched snippet) → read (the archive grid you land in).

## Tone

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: illuminated manuscript meets research archive — quiet premium product film
- Interpretation: few scenes, long holds, soft slides and crossfades; motion is confident, never frantic; bronze is used sparingly as the only bright thing on screen; music stays a warm bed, SFX stay polite.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21.5s (scene clips 4.1 + 7.0 + 5.7 + 5.1, overlapping for crossfades)

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: `#11100d` (lampblack, dark theme) with `#1b1714` umber surfaces
- Accent: `#c7a16a` (antique bronze); on-accent `#1a140f`
- Text: `#f4efe6` heading / `#e7dfd4` body / `#b7ac98` dim; rules `#3a312b`
- Display font: Cormorant Garamond (serif) — headings, headline, italic emphasis
- Body/UI font: Inter (UI) + mono uppercase labels (`ui-monospace`, tracking 0.2em+)
- Strongest visual element: the giant 7%-opacity serif `Å` watermark behind the hero, plus the ruled mono eyebrow and the bronze italic *Istine*.

## Audio direction

- Role: warm bed — restrained, professional, nothing aggressive
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (109.96 BPM, "steady and clean" — the polished/cinematic pick), volume 0.32, gentle fade-in over the first second, holds under the outro then fades out over the last ~1.2s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read (`assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`). Locks: **8.74s** results panel reveal, **17.47s** punch line settle, **19.66s** wordmark. Beat grid for the category cards: 11.46 → 12.02 → 12.55 → 13.11 → 13.64 → 14.20 → 14.73 → 15.29 (one card per beat, whole set held on screen).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the bronze glow and the `Å` watermark breathe on pre-extracted per-frame music bands. No waveform or equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse — one click, one panel landing, two card taps, one bell, plus quiet key ticks on every second typed character
- Audio-coupled moments: per-character key ticks while the query types; one click on `Pretraži članke`; card arrival taps on the first and last category card only; one soft bell on the wordmark
- Restraint rule: no SFX during the headline or the punch line — those two lines get silence around them

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hero headline — 4.1s
Lampblack frame, the giant faded `Å` watermark already present at ~7% opacity. Ruled mono eyebrow `NAUČNA BAZA ZNANJA` draws out from the centre. Serif headline **Uspostavljanje / *Istine* kroz dokaze** rises and settles (Istine bronze italic), then the mono stat line `204 članka · 8 kategorija`. Shared navbar (`ADAM` + nav items) fades in with it.
Sequential/interaction: yes — eyebrow rules extend, then headline lines, then the stat line.
Audio intent: quiet confidence — music fades in, nothing else.
Audio-coupled idea: none; let the sentence land.
Music: vol-12 bed fades in over the first second.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2 (the search field arrives centred, as if the camera moved down to it)

### Scene 2 — Search in action — 7.0s
The search field is the frame, centred, under the mono eyebrow `PRETRAGA`. The query `Meka i Jesrib u Bibliji` types character by character (placeholder `npr. Meka i Jesrib u Bibliji` clears on the first keystroke), `Pretraži članke` presses, and the field rises to the top of the search page while the results resolve below: result row `SPISI / Meka i Jesrib u Bibliji` with the match highlighted in bronze, and the right preview panel under `PronAĐENO U TEKSTU` showing the real snippet *"Postanje 10:30 i naziv Mesa, prevod iz Njukasla iz 1811. koji donosi Meka i Medina…"*.
Sequential/interaction: yes — typing simulated character by character, then a button press, then the results + preview panel arrive as one unit at 8.74s.
Audio intent: tactile and satisfying; the click is the pivot of the whole video.
Audio-coupled idea: `keyboard/keypress-*.wav` on every second character, `ui/mouseclick1` on the submit at 8.5s, soft `interface/drop_001` as the panel lands at 8.74s.
Music: bed continues, energy rises into 8.74s.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The archive — 5.7s
Back to the dark page: mono eyebrow `01 · PREGLEDAJ KATEGORIJE` as on the home grid, the serif stat line `204 članka · 8 kategorija` as the scene headline, then the eight real category cards arrive one per beat with their counts (KURAN 55, ISTORIJA 41, ATEIZAM 40, OPORGOVANJE 24, SPISI 18, AHMEDIJE 11, HADISKE NAUKE 9, HRIŠĆANSTVO 6) in the site's 4×2 card grid, counts in bronze mono.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 8 cards arrive one by one on consecutive beats from 11.46s, the full set is then held.
Audio intent: rhythmic, methodical — an archive filling itself.
Audio-coupled idea: `casino/card-place-*` tap on the first (11.46s) and last (15.29s) card only; counts stay silent.
Music: bed at full presence.
Transition mood: crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Punch line and wordmark — 5.1s
Everything clears to the lampblack frame and the `Å`. The mono line `ZA TRAGAOCE ZA ISTINOM` appears, then the tagline's closing clause lands alone, large, in serif italic: **„u doba organizovane sumnje."** (settled on the 17.47s cue). A beat of stillness, then the **ADAM** wordmark (19.66s cue, bronze rule drawing beneath) and `adam-wiki.github.io/ADAM-Wiki` in mono.
Sequential/interaction: yes — pre-line, punch line, hold, then wordmark + rule + URL.
Audio intent: the one big emotional landing, then a soft close.
Audio-coupled idea: soft `impact/impactBell_heavy_000` at the wordmark, ringing over the fading bed.
Music: bed fades out under the wordmark.
Transition mood: none — end frame.
